The Decision

Datum P4b shipped a schema-conformance test that publishes its own coverage: the 12 fixtures exercise 12 of 40 declared enum values, and two enums — edge.styleAttributes.arrow (0/7) and edge.fromEnd (0/2) — are exercised not at all. Widen the corpus, and how far?
  • What is already guarded. P3's declared-state check catches an enum vanishing, and P4b pins the declared totals, so deleting an enum now fails. Coverage is about catching a narrowing — a legal value quietly dropped.
  • Why the two zero-coverage enums are different. Everything else has at least one value exercised, so a fixture touches it. arrow and fromEnd are touched by nothing — they are the only ones where a partial narrowing is completely invisible.
  • ⛔ Why not bulk-add all 28. The fixtures sit behind the certification gate (12/12) and carry downstream meaning as the Standard's reference corpus. Rewriting them wholesale to satisfy a coverage number is the “editing a disagreement into agreement” move this vault has a standing rule against.
  • ⚠ Honest limit either way. Coverage is a property of the corpus, not of the suite. Whatever we do, the number stays published on the face of the result so it is never read as complete.

Agent's Recommendation

RATIONALE

Partial. Add fixture coverage for arrow and fromEnd only — the two places where a narrowing is currently invisible — and leave the other 28 values as published, named, accepted exposure.

KEY FACTORS

  • The zero-coverage pair is the only genuinely blind spot
  • Deletion is already caught by the declared-totals pin
  • Touching the reference corpus has downstream consequences

RISKS

  • Even after this, 26 of 40 values remain unexercised — the published figure moves but does not disappear
