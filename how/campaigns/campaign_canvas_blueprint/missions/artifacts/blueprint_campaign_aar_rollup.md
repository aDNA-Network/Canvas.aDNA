# Operation Blueprint — campaign AAR rollup (2026-09-09)

Chartered 2026-08-22 · amended 2026-09-07 (+P2c, dated scope amendment) · completed 2026-09-09.
**7 phases, 6 missions, 9 sessions.** Thesis proven in all three directions it claimed: the canvas
is the fleet's diagrammatic-context substrate (P1/P2), the federation surface that carries it is now

## The one finding this campaign was really about

Blueprint set out to fix a *federation* problem and spent most of its budget discovering an
*epistemic* one. Nine times, across every phase and in both directions, the defect was the same:

> ### ⭐ A stated fact nobody re-derived.
> Re-reading never catches these. Only re-deriving does.

| # | Phase | The stated fact | What measurement said |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-P2-8 | P2c | "6 published files" | **7** — and this is the record that *established* the practice |
| F-P2b-1 | P2b | "5 Operations canvases diverged" | **10**, and 2 of 5 pairs are byte-different / semantically identical |
| F-P2b-3 | P2b | "29 bare ScienceStanley files" | **33**, in three classes, **9 of them our own** archived producer's output |
| F-P3-1 | P3 | "14 wrapper vaults" | **15** — WGS adopted 2026-08-10 and was never indexed |
| F-P3-2 | P3 | "Oration: wrapper NONE, the 🔴 G7 blocker" | **adopted the day we asked** — five weeks reporting a closed gap as open |
| F-P3-4 | P3 | "adopt VisualDNA's lockstep-flip mechanics" | `skill_lockstep_flip` **was never built** — a dependency on a dead referent |
| F-P3-7 | P3 | a peer's premise about `conformance_level` | **wrong** — and they had reversed a correct ruling on it |
| F-P4-1 | P4 | "window-scoped capture is impossible here" | **false when written** — the instrument had been on this node for 15 months, already assigned to this vault |
| F-P5-3 | P5 | "all gates green" ×3 closes | true of the six suites listed; **`canvas_context` had left the list** and was red for two days |

**The two most expensive were the two that sounded most like conclusions**, not figures. F-P4-1 was
a *sentence* — "whole-screen capture is ruled out, it caught a third party's private messages" —
every word true, and the conclusion drawn from it false. It blocked a mandatory gate through **four
phases**. F-P3-2 was an index row, and it misreported a peer's cooperation as a refusal for five
weeks.

⇒ The practice adopted and now cited fleet-wide (Hopper ratified the generic form as their ADR-011
A8 §5): ***state the population on the face of the number — tip or history · class or literal ·
tracked or working-tree.*** P3 re-derived all 11 of its published census figures at close and every
one reproduced. P5 re-derived the two figures it inherited (SS's 5-and-21, Vulcan's 12) and both
reproduced. **That is what the practice looks like when it works, and it is worth as much as the
catches.**

## The five other findings that should outlive the campaign

1. **⭐ A registry defines its own blind spot in its membership rule** (F-P3, §1b). `federation_index`
   answered *"who holds a wrapper?"* — so **10 vaults emitting 49 canvases with no seam at all**, the
   least-supervised surface in the fleet, were invisible **by construction**. No amount of diligent
   maintenance would have surfaced them; a peer had to say it about their own vault: *"the clean tree
   is clean by low traffic, not by construction."* Ask what your index cannot represent, not just
   whether it is current.

2. **⭐ A governance rule that reads as principled is more dangerous than one that reads as
   arbitrary, because nobody audits it** (F-P2-13). The predicate `graduated: false and accepted == 0`
   cited real registry fields, read defensibly, and would have made **13 of 14 traps non-gating** —
   including one that fired 55 times in the session that wrote it. It failed because the III
   `cycles_*` counters are unmaintained, so `fired == 0` means *no record*, not *never useful*. It
   was caught by auditing the fields before trusting them.

3. **⭐ An uncollected reply is a finding you will pay full price to rediscover** (F-P3-6). Kennedy's
   reply sat `staged_unsent` in **their** outbox for 34 days. Its closing paragraph reported the
   `CV-LEAD-COST-01`→`CV-HIERARCHY-01` guidance-vs-trap conflict on 2026-08-04; we rediscovered and
   fixed it independently as **F-P2-9** on 2026-09-07. Another of its findings had shipped *the day
   before they wrote it*, built partly from their own contributed tool. *Nobody was wrong; the
   channel was.* This is the argument for `who/coordination/inbox/`, made in arrears — and the box,
   opened at P2, has since caught three inbound memos including one that landed mid-session.

4. **⭐ An honest measurement admits true findings, not just removes false ones** (F-P2-11). Correct
   heights exposed a CRITICAL page overflow that had always been real; restoring 3 missing PNGs
   turned `file_missing` into `aspect_drift`; correct manifest-relative resolution turned a printed
   `0 findings [OK]` into checks that had been **skipping**. A measurement that only ever makes your
   numbers better is not a measurement.

5. **⭐ A mechanical fix delivered as a whole-file rewrite is not mechanical to the person reviewing
   it** (F-P5-2). `normalize_edges` is exactly as mechanical as we told ScienceStanley. The
   *serialization* is not: `json.dumps(indent=2)` turned a 21-key change into **1436 changed lines**,
   in an artifact they land under their own commit. Rewritten as a textual insertion: **42 lines**.
   The tool was right; the handover was not, and only the recipient's diff would have shown it.

**And one about the shape of the work**, which is P4's whole argument: **build the consumer first.**
The plan said doing so would turn spec §3 from a paragraph into five fixtures an emitter can run
against. ComfyUI's operator then ruled their emitter *forward off its blocking milestone* **on
reading those fixtures**, and it shipped the same sitting — validating 12/12, which we re-derived
here as 15/15. A spec that can be run against is a different object from a spec that can be read.

## What was NOT done, and why that is the right answer

- **The H4 live chain did not run and spend was never requested.** Two independent reasons held all
  campaign: their emitter did not exist, and their venue node was flapping. Both are now resolved on
  their side — and it is *still* not asked, because the venue manifest (their M-RD1) has not landed,
  and both vaults agree the ask is joint when it does. Five fixtures and one real 20-variant batch
  prove more than a hand-written manifest over borrowed pixels would.
- **No schema change.** The default locked at charter held for seven phases. LIP-0010 (`authority`
  as a validated enum) was assessed, **recommended, and deferred** on Rosetta's ruling. Firewall
  `git diff --stat -- what/code/canvas_std/` = **0** at every single gate.
- **No conversion performed in a peer's tree.** Every offer was an offer; every peer vault was
  read-only; every memo was left untracked for the recipient's own read-receipt commit.
- **The M-PL3 ruling was not taken.** The charter said "ruling"; ScienceStanley asked for a joint
  sitting and was right to. Dossier staged, decision open.
- **`_reserved` uplift was never shipped to a consumer.** Held on `b1.5` through three phases because
  no `authority` value honestly fits a hand-authored canvas — and `canvas_std` accepts an invented
  one **silently** (F-B1-2). We nearly shipped `view` into two vaults (F-P2b-5). ⇒ ***green on an
  unvalidated key is silence, not evidence.***
