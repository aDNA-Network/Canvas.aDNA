## §1 · Ruled 2026-09-11, and the shape is better than the one you were promised

aDNA.aDNA operator-ruled **adopt**, amended by our own erratum E2. The single three-value axis is
**split into two independent ones**:

| Axis | The question it answers | Values |
|---|---|---|
| **`authority`** | *Who owns the meaning?* | `dual_channel` · `view` |
| **`production`** | *How is the picture made?* | `hand_authored` · `generated` |

**The placeholder problem is gone**, and not by finding a fourth value. Your review boards are
**hand-authored primary artifacts**: no `.lattice.yaml`, nothing generated them, no prose twin. Ruled
from the pattern's own text, they are **outside its scope entirely** — it governs *"a `.canvas`
**beside a document**"*, and a board **is** the document. Nothing else owns its meaning, so the
authority question does not arise, and the pattern explicitly declines to call an undeclared canvas
nonconformant.

So the honest block for a board is `production: "hand_authored"` with **`authority` absent** — not
empty, not a placeholder. Our tooling emits it that way by construction now.

⇒ **When your live batch arrives, it gets a value that is true, and one that will not migrate.**
